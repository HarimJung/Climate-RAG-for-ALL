# Homepage Redesign Skill

## Goal
Make the homepage immediately communicate the depth, trustworthiness, and usefulness of Visual Climate.

## Homepage Priorities
The homepage must make users understand:
1. what Visual Climate is
2. what they get from searching a country
3. why the scores are trustworthy
4. who the product is for
5. what to do next

## Recommended Section Order
1. Hero
2. Trust stats
3. What you get in 10 seconds
4. Who it's for
5. Why trust this
6. System behind the scores
7. Posters / guides / reports entry points
8. Footer with methodology and source access

## Hero Requirements
Must include:
- strong headline
- clear subheadline
- country search as main CTA
- secondary CTA to sample report
- visible sample result preview
- trust stats near the fold

## Trust Stats
Highlight:
- countries covered
- indicators tracked
- domains scored
- public data sources
- free shareable outputs

## What You Get
Show:
- overall grade
- classification
- domain breakdown
- plain-language summary
- download-ready poster
- deep-dive path

## Who It's For
Include user groups:
- teachers/students
- journalists/researchers
- NGOs/policy teams
- sustainability/disclosure teams

## Why Trust This
Must surface:
- public sources
- transparent methodology
- cross-verification
- clear update logic
- open / documented approach where applicable

## System Behind the Scores
Translate technical complexity into user value:
- collect
- validate
- analyze
- score
- visualize
- publish

Do not make this section feel like technical bragging.

## UX Rules
- premium and minimal, but not empty
- data-rich, but easy to scan
- clear CTA hierarchy
- strong use of cards
- interpretive copy everywhere needed
- never show abstract labels without explanation
